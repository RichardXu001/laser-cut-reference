Glossary

Dross
Resolidified metal clinging to the underside of a cut, usually from too little gas pressure or too much heat.
Kerf
The width of material removed by the beam; typically 0.2–0.5 mm for fiber lasers.
Heat-affected zone (HAZ)
The narrow band beside the cut where the metal's microstructure changed from the heat.
Pierce
The initial hole burned through the sheet before the cut contour starts; far slower than cutting and often the bottleneck on thick plate.
Taper
Difference between cut width at the top and bottom of the edge; expressed as an angle or a perpendicularity tolerance.
Striations
The fine lines left on a cut edge by the beam's advance; their spacing and roughness define ISO 9013 class.
Assist gas
Oxygen, nitrogen or air blown through the nozzle to eject molten metal and, for oxygen, to add heat.
Focal point
Where the beam is narrowest; its position relative to the surface controls kerf width and edge quality.
Nozzle
The copper tip that shapes the gas flow; a worn nozzle changes cut quality before it fails completely.
Nesting
Arranging parts on a sheet to minimise scrap; software-driven and a major cost lever.

For a wider set of terms with process context, see the laser cutting glossary on the main site.
