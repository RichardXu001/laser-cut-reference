Thickness Limits: Brochure vs Production

The maximum thickness in a spec sheet is an edge case. Production thickness is what matters.

Machine brochures quote a maximum thickness. Shops need a production thickness - the thickest section they can cut repeatedly with a sellable edge.

The 60-70 percent rule

Production thickness is typically 60-70 percent of the brochure maximum. A 6 kW machine quoted at 20 mm on mild steel is realistically a 12-16 mm production machine.

Why the gap

At maximum thickness the cut is slow, the edge is rough, dross is likely, and the process is far from economical. Nobody quotes a job at that speed.
