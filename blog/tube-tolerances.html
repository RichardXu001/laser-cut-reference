Tube Cutting: Tolerances and Chuck Types

Tube lasers cut profiles in 3D; tolerance depends on the chuck, the support and the software.

Tube cutting adds rotation to the process. The tolerances that matter are not just the kerf - they include the chuck runout and the tail support.

Chuck types

Three-jaw, four-jaw and specialized chucks trade setup time against precision. Heavy tube needs a chuck rated for the weight and a support that follows the section.

The software side

Tube CAD unfolds the 3D shape into a cuttable path. Check that the machine's software handles your file formats and that operators can import and nest.
