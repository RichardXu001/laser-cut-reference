Dross on the Underside: Causes and Fixes

Dross is a symptom. The fix depends on which of the three common causes you have.

Dross - resolidified metal clinging to the underside of the cut - is the most common cut quality complaint. It has three usual causes.

1. Insufficient gas pressure

Molten metal cannot leave the kerf fast enough. Raise pressure or check the nozzle for wear.

2. Wrong focus position

The beam should be focused near the bottom of the plate for thick material. An offset focus widens the kerf and leaves dross.

3. Too much heat

Excess power or too slow a feed overheats the melt, and it clings. Speed up or reduce power.

The Fabricator and machine builders publish troubleshooting charts; the fault-finding order above resolves most cases.
