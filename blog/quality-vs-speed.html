Cut Quality vs Speed: The Trade-Off Curve

Every cut has a quality-speed curve. Knowing where you sit on it explains most cost differences.

For any material and thickness there is a curve: slower cuts give better edges, faster cuts trade quality for throughput. The optimum is where the required quality meets the best speed.

Where shops go wrong

Two errors are common: running too slow (paying for quality nobody asked for) and running too fast (producing dross that requires a second operation). Both raise cost per part.

Finding the point

Start from the drawing's class, then test the fastest speed that holds it. Store that as the parameter set for the material and thickness.
