Piercing Thick Plate: Why It Slows the Job

The pierce is often the slowest minute of a thick-plate job. Understanding it changes how you quote.

Before a contour, the beam must burn a hole through the plate. On thin sheet that takes a fraction of a second; on thick plate it can take several seconds per pierce.

What affects pierce time

  • Thickness and material.
  • Gas and pressure.
  • Pierce method: single-stage, multi-stage, or pre-drilled.

Quoting impact

A job with a hundred small parts on 15 mm plate can spend more time piercing than cutting. Nesting software usually reports pierce count; use it.

Machine builders and The Fabricator publish pierce-time guidance for heavy plate.
