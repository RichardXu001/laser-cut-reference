Laser Cutting Aluminum: Absorption and Oxide

Aluminum cuts well on fiber lasers - if you account for reflection and the oxide layer.

Aluminum reflects near-infrared light more than steel and conducts heat away quickly. Fiber lasers still cut it well, but the settings differ.

Absorption

Once the cut starts, absorption rises sharply. The challenge is starting the pierce without reflecting too much energy back into the optics.

Oxide layer

Aluminum carries an oxide layer that melts at a much higher temperature than the metal. Clean or brush the surface where a reliable pierce matters, especially on thick sections.

Gas

Nitrogen gives a clean edge; air works for non-critical parts. See the gas selection reference.
