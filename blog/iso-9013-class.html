What ISO 9013 Quality Class Should You Specify?

Class numbers decide what edge a supplier can hand over. Here is how to pick one without over- or under-specifying.

ISO 9013 defines thermal cut quality by perpendicularity and roughness. The class you write on the drawing determines what the shop must deliver - and what it may reject as out of tolerance.

Start from the function

Visible edges on consumer products need a tighter class than structural parts that get painted or welded. Specify the class from the part function, not from the machine's best claim.

The ladder

Lower class numbers mean straighter edges. Classes around 1-2 are for visible or weld-critical edges; 2-4 covers general fabrication; 3-5 is for non-visible structural work.

Ranges in this article are typical builder ranges; verify against the current ISO 9013 text before writing a specification.
