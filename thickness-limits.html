Thickness Limits

"Maximum thickness" in a brochure is a laboratory edge case. The number that matters is the thickest section a shop can cut at production speed with acceptable edge quality — usually 60–70% of the brochure maximum.

PowerMild steel (O₂)Stainless (N₂)Aluminum (N₂)Copper/brass
1.5 kW4 mm3 mm3 mm1.5 mm
2 kW6 mm4 mm4 mm2 mm
3 kW8–10 mm6–8 mm5–6 mm3 mm
6 kW12–16 mm10–12 mm10–12 mm4–6 mm
12 kW25–30 mm16–20 mm16–20 mm8 mm

What limits the thickness

  • Beam absorption — copper and brass absorb poorly at 1064 nm, so they are limited well before steel at the same power.
  • Kerf ejection — below a certain pressure, molten metal cannot leave the kerf fast enough and dross forms on the underside.
  • Edge quality — the cut may complete at 20 mm on a 6 kW machine, but with taper and rough striations that fail most drawings.

Ranges vary by machine builder and gas supply. For process settings, see the thickness and speed chart.
